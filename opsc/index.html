Is Crack-X OPSC free?

Yes. Every feature is available on the free plan with a limited amount of content and practice each day or month. When you want more questions, tests or topics, you can upgrade from the pricing page.

Is the content verified?

Every topic is drafted from official and standard sources, fact-checked, and approved by our team before it is published. Practice questions show whether they are official previous-year questions or practice items.

Can I use the same account for another exam?

Each Crack-X exam product has its own account, so your dashboard, progress and plan stay focused on one exam. You can use the same email address for another exam's account.

Does the timer and marking match the real exam?

The simulator uses the marking scheme from the latest official notification. Until a scheme is verified, the test shows a clear "unverified" label.
