Why the content can be trusted

Crack-X is as much a content system as an app. Every topic goes through the same auditable pipeline before students see it.

  1. 1/4

    Official sources

    Syllabi, notifications and papers collected only from allowlisted government domains.

  2. 2/4

    Original drafting

    Notes, cards and questions written from research briefs. Every claim cites a source.

  3. 3/4

    Independent fact-check

    A separate reviewer on a different model checks every claim and answer key.

  4. 4/4

    Human approval

    Nothing is published until an editor approves it in the admin panel.
