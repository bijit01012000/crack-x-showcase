  1. Q1PracticeUnverified key

    Consider the following statements regarding Article 74:

    1. There shall be a Council of Ministers with the Prime Minister at the head to aid and advise the

    President.

    2. The President may act contrary to the advice of the Council of Ministers after reconsideration.

    3. The President may require the Council of Ministers to reconsider its advice.

    4. After reconsideration, the President is constitutionally bound to act in accordance with the advice

    tendered.

    How many of the above statements are correct?
